# TM-compatibility supplement for original and rematch move candidates

**Project:** Pokémon Lightning Yellow. **Status: conditional technical draft, NOT implementation-ready.** Built from current FireRed-derived `src/data/pokemon/tmhm_learnsets.h`, `src/data/battle_moves.h` and `src/data/pokemon/species_info.h`. The approved 252-species ability/move/TM audit is a separate evolving project and may supersede these current-source assignments.

This supplement targets the party slots in `ordinary-trainers-learnset-moves-originals.md` and `ordinary-trainers-learnset-moves-rematches.md` that lacked a reliable damaging same-type move in the available level-up pool. It finds one or two STAB machine moves the **present source code** says the species can learn. It does not prove TM acquisition, event-level fairness, the owner's final TM move substitution tables, custom typing consistency, or strong four-move balance. No TM here is authorized for coding until those checks pass.

**Power gate:** below level 21 do not add a machine; Lv21-29 consider lower-power early machines only (03, 09, 28, 34, 39, 40, 43, power up to 60); Lv30-39 power up to 90; Lv40-49 power up to 110; later levels current-source legal TMs. These are conservative candidate ceilings, not approved access rules. Existing owner-approved Pokémon species never change.
